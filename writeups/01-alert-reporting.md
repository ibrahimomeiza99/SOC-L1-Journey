
# TryHackMe: Alert Reporting

## Overview
Soemtimes as a junior SOC analyst in a company, some alerts would need immediate
attention of a more superior analyst to work on, the ability of writing a concise
and well detailed report makes you standout and efficient at the work that you do.
This lab/room taught me three important concept as a SOC L1 analyst which are
how to effectively deal with writing alert report, escalating alerts, and finally 
effective communication.

## Scenario
In this lab/room, I was given two scenerios, one of which is a "Email Marked as Phishing after Delivery"
where I have to investigate the incident and figure out wheather or not the email received in malicious.
The other scenerio was an investigation on a host regarding "Spike of Domain Discovery Commands" where arbitrary commands where being
executed.

## My Approach
1. I looked at the description of the alert to have an idea of what the whole incident is
2. I used the tryhackme SOC dashboard to view and triage the alert
3. After discovering that the alert is a TP, I escalated it to L2

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Alert triage
- Enrichment
- report writing
- Communication
